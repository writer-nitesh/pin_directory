Whether you need to send a parcel, get a money order, or renew a postal savings account, finding the **nearest post office** in India can be done in multiple ways. This guide covers all methods from GPS detection to the official India Post locator.

---

## Method 1: GPS-Based Post Office Finder (Instant)

The fastest way to find the closest post office:

1. Open the [Find My PIN Code GPS Tool](/find-my-pincode)
2. Click **"Use My Current Location"** and allow location access
3. The tool will return the **nearest delivery post office**, its PIN code, district, and state within seconds

This uses your device GPS coordinates to locate the nearest India Post delivery area. No address typing needed.

---

## Method 2: Search by PIN Code

If you already know your area''s PIN code:

1. Go to [Pin Directory](/) and enter the 6-digit PIN code
2. View the **full list of post offices** registered under that PIN code
3. Each post office shows its name, delivery status (Delivery/Non-Delivery), and division

Example: Entering [110001](/pincode/110001) shows all post offices in the New Delhi GPO area.

---

## Method 3: Search by Area Name

1. Type your **city, area name, or locality** into the [Pin Directory search bar](/)
2. Select the matching result
3. See all post offices under that area''s PIN code

---

## Method 4: Official India Post Office Locator

The [India Post Post Office Locator](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/locate-postoffice) lets you search by:
- **By PIN Code** — enter any 6-digit code
- **By State & District** — browse hierarchically
- **By Office Name** — if you know the post office name

This is the most comprehensive official database.

## Types of Post Offices and Their Services

Not all post offices offer the same services. Here''s what each type typically provides:

| Type | Services Available | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **GPO** (General Post Office) | All services including international mail, Speed Post, India Post Payments Bank | Major city centre |
| **HPO** (Head Post Office) | All services, district-level financial services, bulk mail acceptance | District headquarters |
| **SO** (Sub Post Office) | Letters, parcels, Speed Post, money orders, postal savings | Urban neighbourhoods, towns |
| **BO** (Branch Office) | Basic mail acceptance, India Post Savings Account | Rural villages, peri-urban areas |

---

## Common Services at India Post Offices

- **Speed Post** — Express domestic and international delivery
- **Registered Post** — Tracked letter and parcel service with acknowledgement
- **Money Orders (MO)** — Domestic fund transfer through the postal network
- **India Post Payments Bank (IPPB)** — Banking services at select post offices
- **PLI / RPLI** — Postal Life Insurance (for government/PSU employees and rural citizens)
- **Passport Application** — Select post offices act as Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) centres

---

## Post Office Timings

Most India Post offices operate:
- **Head Post Offices (HPO)**: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday (closed Sunday & public holidays)
- **Sub Post Offices (SO)**: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday
- **Branch Offices (BO)**: Often 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM or 3:00 PM, limited days per week

Timings may vary by state and season. Confirm with your specific post office for accurate hours.
