India Post divides the nation into **9 administrative postal zones** comprising 23 postal circles. Each circle is typically headed by a Principal Chief Postmaster General.

|---|---|---|

### Types of Post Offices in India

- **GPO (General Post Office)**: The central postal hub of a major metropolitan city or circle.
- **HPO (Head Post Office)**: The primary administrative and financial post office of a district or division.
- **SO (Sub Post Office)**: Established in urban neighborhoods and large towns, handling all standard postal services.
- **BO (Branch Office)**: Located primarily in rural villages and peri-urban areas, providing essential mail and small savings services.
