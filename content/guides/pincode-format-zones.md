[India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in) divides the nation into **9 administrative postal zones** comprising **23 postal circles**. Each circle is typically headed by a **Principal Chief Postmaster General (PCPMG)**. The first digit of every 6-digit PIN code directly corresponds to one of these 9 zones.

## Types of Post Offices in India

India Post classifies its post offices into 4 functional types:

- **GPO (General Post Office)**: The central postal hub of a major metropolitan city or circle. Examples: New Delhi GPO (110001), Mumbai GPO (400001), Kolkata GPO (700001).
- **HPO (Head Post Office)**: The primary administrative and financial post office of a district or division. Handles money orders, registered mail, and speed post at district level.
- **SO (Sub Post Office)**: Established in urban neighbourhoods and large towns, handling all standard postal services — letters, parcels, speed post, and postal savings.
- **BO (Branch Office)**: Located primarily in rural villages and peri-urban areas, providing essential mail and small savings services. Most BOs operate 3–5 days per week.

## How Zones Relate to PIN Codes

The first digit of any 6-digit PIN code maps directly to one of the 9 zones above. For example:
- **PIN 400001** → first digit `4` → Zone 4 → Maharashtra → Mumbai GPO
- **PIN 600001** → first digit `6` → Zone 6 → Tamil Nadu → Chennai GPO
- **PIN 700001** → first digit `7` → Zone 7 → West Bengal → Kolkata GPO
