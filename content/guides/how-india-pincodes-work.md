Every 6-digit Indian PIN code follows a strict hierarchical geographic structure. By reading the digits from left to right, you can trace a package from the macro region down to the local doorstep.

### Anatomy of a 6-Digit PIN Code

Consider the PIN code **110001** (New Delhi GPO):

```text
  1          1          0          0          0          1
[Zone]  [Sub-Zone]  [District]  [------ Delivery Office ------]
```

#### 1. First Digit: Postal Zone

The first digit indicates one of the 9 geographic postal zones across India:

- **1**: Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Chandigarh
- **2**: Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand
- **3**: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Daman & Diu, Dadra & Nagar Haveli
- **4**: Maharashtra, Goa, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh
- **5**: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka
- **6**: Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Puducherry, Lakshadweep
- **7**: West Bengal, Odisha, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Meghalaya, Andaman & Nicobar
- **8**: Bihar, Jharkhand
- **9**: Army Postal Service (APS) & Field Post Offices (FPO)

#### 2. Second Digit: Sub-Zone / State

When combined with the first digit, the second digit pinpoints the specific state, union territory, or revenue circle. For instance:
- `11` = National Capital Territory of Delhi
- `56` = Bangalore Urban & Rural region in Karnataka
- `40` = Mumbai metropolitan circle in Maharashtra

#### 3. Third Digit: Sorting District

The third digit combined with the first two indicates the sorting district or postal revenue division. In urban centers, this corresponds to major railway and air mail transit hubs where bulk mail is unpacked and segregated.

#### 4. Fourth to Sixth Digits: Delivery Office

The last three digits represent the specific destination delivery post office.
- Digits like `001` typically denote the primary **General Post Office (GPO)** or **Head Post Office (HPO)** of the city or district.
- Other combinations identify **Sub Post Offices (SO)** or rural **Branch Post Offices (BO)** assigned to deliver mail to individual streets and villages.
