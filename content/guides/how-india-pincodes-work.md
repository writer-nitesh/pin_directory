Every 6-digit Indian PIN code follows a strict hierarchical geographic structure defined by [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in). By reading the digits from left to right, you can trace a package from the macro region all the way down to the local doorstep.

---

## Anatomy of a 6-Digit PIN Code

Consider the PIN code **110001** (New Delhi GPO):

```text
  1          1          0          0          0          1
[Zone]  [Sub-Zone]  [District]  [------ Delivery Office ------]
```

Each digit (or group of digits) narrows the geographic scope one level further, from national zone → state → sorting district → specific post office.

---

## Digit-by-Digit Breakdown

### 1st Digit: Postal Zone (1–9)

The first digit indicates one of 9 geographic postal zones across India:

| Zone | States & Territories Covered |
|---|---|
| **1** | Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Chandigarh |
| **2** | Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand |
| **3** | Rajasthan, Gujarat, Daman & Diu, Dadra & Nagar Haveli |
| **4** | Maharashtra, Goa, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh |
| **5** | Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka |
| **6** | Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Puducherry, Lakshadweep |
| **7** | West Bengal, Odisha, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Meghalaya, Andaman & Nicobar |
| **8** | Bihar, Jharkhand |
| **9** | Army Postal Service (APS) & Field Post Offices (FPO) |

For a detailed explanation of what each zone covers, see our [Postal Zones Guide](/guides/pincode-format-zones).

---

### 2nd Digit: Sub-Zone / State Identifier

When combined with the first digit, the second digit pinpoints the specific state, union territory, or revenue circle. Common examples:

- `11` = National Capital Territory of Delhi
- `12` or `13` = Haryana and parts of Punjab
- `40` = Mumbai metropolitan circle in Maharashtra
- `56` = Bangalore Urban & Rural region in Karnataka
- `60` = Chennai / Tamil Nadu

---

### 3rd Digit: Sorting District

The third digit combined with the first two indicates the **sorting district** or postal revenue division. In urban centres, this corresponds to major railway and air mail transit hubs where bulk mail is unpacked and segregated before onward distribution.

For example:
- `110` = Delhi sorting circle (covers central Delhi)
- `400` = Mumbai sorting hub
- `560` = Bangalore division

---

### 4th to 6th Digits: Delivery Post Office

The final three digits identify the **specific destination delivery post office**:

- **`001`** typically denotes the primary **General Post Office (GPO)** or **Head Post Office (HPO)** of the city or district.
- Other combinations identify **Sub Post Offices (SO)** or rural **Branch Post Offices (BO)** assigned to deliver mail to individual streets and villages.
- Higher numbers (e.g., `085`, `110`) usually indicate more suburban or rural branch offices within the same sorting district.
